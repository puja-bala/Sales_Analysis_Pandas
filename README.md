# Sales Analysis with Python Pandas
Set of real world data analysis completed using the Python Pandas library.

## Background Information:

In this project I used Python Libraries Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn to analyze and answer business questions about 12 months of sales data. The data contains hundreds of thousands of electronics purchases broken down by month, product type, cost, purchase address, etc. 

## 5 high level business questions related to our data:

1. **What was the best month for sales? How much was earned that month?**
2. **What city sold the most product?**
3. **What time should we display advertisemens to maximize the likelihood of customer’s buying product?**
4. **What products are most often sold together?**
5. **What product sold the most? Why do you think it sold the most?**
